What is Criminal Profiling?

Criminal profiling is a crucial technique used by law enforcement to analyze offender behavior, identify crime patterns, and predict future criminal activities. Traditional profiling methods rely heavily on human intuition, which can introduce bias and inconsistencies. The Crime Type Prediction model classifies crimes into categories such as Theft, Fraud, or Assault based on crime scene details, offender characteristics, and location data. The Crime Severity Prediction model assesses the seriousness of a crime (Low, Medium, or High) by analyzing weapon involvement, injury severity, and financial damage. Additionally, the Offender Risk Level Prediction model evaluates whether an individual is at Low, Medium, or High risk of reoffending using psychological, behavioral, and past criminal records.With interactive dashboards, crime heatmaps, and AI-generated reports, this system provides a cutting-edge, data-driven approach to criminal investigations, improving both efficiency and public safety.
